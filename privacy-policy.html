Privacy Policy for Kids Drawing: Toddler Paint

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

Kids Drawing: Toddler Paint is committed to protecting the privacy of children and all users. Our application complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K, and Google Play Families Policies.

2. Information Collection & Usage

We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII). Any drawings, sketches, or artwork created by the user remain stored locally on the device and are never uploaded to any remote server.

3. Advertising & Child-Directed Content

Our app displays child-safe advertisements through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). All ads conform to strict Google Play Families Policy guidelines:

  • All ad requests are flagged with tagForChildDirectedTreatment: true.
  • Maximum ad content rating is restricted to G (General Audiences).
  • Zero behavioral targeting or personalized profiling (npa: '1' enabled).
  • Interactive interstitial ads are protected behind an adult arithmetic Parental Gate.

4. Permissions

The app only requests standard permissions strictly required for offline drawing canvas functionality and saving images locally to user device storage upon explicit request.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this policy, please reach out via GitHub: https://github.com/navyask911/kids-drawing-toddler-paint
